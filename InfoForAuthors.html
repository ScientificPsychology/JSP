Manuscript Submission Instructions

Types of Articles Accepted

The Journal of Scientific Psychology accepts submissions of the following types:

  • Original empirical works
  • Original theoretical works
  • Comments on published works
  • Book and movie reviews

Manuscript Preparation

Manuscripts should be prepared in APA format as described in the Publication Manual of the American Psychological Association (6th ed.). All manuscripts must be double‑spaced and must include figures and references (in proper APA format) on a separate page. Tables may be included within the text when appropriate for APA style.

As this is an online publication, the use of color is welcome and encouraged. Authors may consult APA’s manuscript preparation checklist at: http://www.apa.org/journals/authors/manuscript_check.html

All manuscripts must include a 150‑word abstract on a separate page, along with five keywords to facilitate indexing and search engine recognition. The manuscript may not exceed 50 double‑spaced pages in total length, including the cover page, abstract, references, tables, and figures.

Review Process

All submissions must include a cover letter indicating that the manuscript is being submitted for consideration and that APA ethical guidelines have been followed: http://www.apa.org/ethics/.

The cover letter must include all contact and institutional information (name, institution, date of submission). All identifying information should be removed from the manuscript itself to ensure masked review.

Each manuscript is reviewed by three reviewers: two professional reviewers and one student reviewer.

Permissions

Authors are responsible for obtaining all necessary permissions to reproduce copyrighted materials in electronic form. This includes test materials, photographs, and any other copyrighted content.

Submission

Manuscripts should be submitted by email to the Editor in PDF, Rich Text Format (.rtf), or Microsoft Word (.doc). The Editor may be reached HERE.

Authors should retain a personal copy of the manuscript. Confirmation of acceptance for review will be provided within seven business days of submission.

Important Note

APA policy prohibits authors from submitting the same manuscript for concurrent consideration by multiple publications. Manuscripts that have been published in whole or in substantial part elsewhere are not eligible for publication. Authors should consult journal editors if there is any question regarding prior publication.
